Biological Trabecular Remodeling (Method A)

Bone adapts to mechanical loads via cellular mechanotransduction (Wolff's Law). Osteoclasts resorb bone trabeculae experiencing low mechanical stimulus (strain energy density), while osteoblasts deposit bone along principal stress trajectories, converging to lightweight stretch-dominated topologies in very few iterations.

Darwinian Evolutionary Optimization (Method B)

Genetic algorithms simulate biological evolution through population variation, crossover, and random point mutation over discrete gauge thicknesses. While capable of avoiding local minima, combinatorial evolution requires orders of magnitude more evaluations than remodeling-driven resorption.
